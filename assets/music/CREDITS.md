# Built-in tunes

The tunes in this folder play in the video editor and in saved videos. All of them are free to use, including commercially, and none needs a credit in a video.

`scripts/make-music.py` rebuilds every file from the sources below: it trims silence, brings each tune to about −16 LUFS (a lookahead limiter lowers the loudest peaks of quiet, dynamic tunes by at most 5 dB) and saves it as AAC at 128 kbps.

## From FreePD (public domain, CC0 1.0)

FreePD published these recordings under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 public domain dedication. Its site footer read: "Free for Commercial Use, Free Of Royalties, Free Of Attribution, Creative Commons 0", with the note that the music is "copyright free" to the extent that the law allows. The site went offline in 2026, so the Internet Archive's copies are the record: the page each tune was listed on, the MP3 it was made from and the MP3's SHA-256 hash. FreePD's [legal page](https://web.archive.org/web/20251202055533/https://freepd.com/legal.php) explains its approach to copyright.

An AudioSet sound classifier found no singing in these recordings.

## Made for the app

- **Happy Birthday**: `birthday-happy-birthday.m4a`
  - The melody of "Happy Birthday to You" (Mildred J. Hill and Patty Smith Hill, 1893) is in the public domain.
  - Arranged as a waltz for the app in `scripts/make-music.py` and rendered with FluidSynth and the [GeneralUser GS](https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser) sound bank by S. Christian Collins, whose licence allows any use of music made with it, including commercial use.
